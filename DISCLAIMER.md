# Research, clinical and project-identity disclaimer

Fast OFM is a research prototype. It has not been clinically validated,
certified as a medical device or qualified for diagnostic use. Images and
measurements produced by the project must not be used as the sole basis for
clinical decisions.

Fast OFM is independent from the OpenFlexure project and does not imply
OpenFlexure endorsement. The modified server repository preserves the
OpenFlexure license, identity and provenance because OpenFlexure is its upstream
base.

The system moves motors, controls illumination and handles electrical hardware.
Incorrect wiring, current limits, pin assignments, travel bounds or calibration
can cause damage. Verify each machine independently and maintain a physical
power-removal path.

Software and documentation are provided under their repository licenses,
without warranty.
