# Authors and contributors

Fast OFM is developed and released by **Alexander Fridman**. His work in this
checkpoint covers the controller and OpenFlexure integration,
motor-control software, illumination control, calibration and RG autofocus,
sparse focus prediction, scan orchestration, stitching acceleration and WSI
output.

The prototype uses a custom Cartesian linear stage developed within the wider
prototype effort before the controller and imaging integration documented
here. The mechanical contributor is not named in this release; this snapshot
does not claim Alexander Fridman's authorship of that underlying mechanical
design.

The current microscope integration is built on the OpenFlexure Microscope
Server. Upstream OpenFlexure authors and contributors are credited in the
`openflexure-wsi` repository and its immutable upstream history.
